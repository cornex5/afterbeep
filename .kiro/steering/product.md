# Afterbeep: Product

## What it is

Afterbeep is an answering machine for small businesses. When nobody picks up, Afterbeep answers the call, records the caller, transcribes what they said, and extracts a structured message card. The owner gets the card by email within seconds.

Target customers: tradespeople (plumbers, electricians, carpenters), clinics and practices, independent shops, any small operation where a real human cannot always answer the phone.

## The three core promises

**1. Never lose a caller.**
Every call that reaches Afterbeep is recorded and processed. No call silently disappears. If the pipeline fails at any step, the raw audio and a failure notice are preserved so the owner can follow up manually.

**2. Never invent a phone number.**
The callback number in the message card is taken only from what the caller spoke or from verified caller ID. If neither source produces a number, the card shows "unknown" for that field. Afterbeep never guesses, never constructs, and never hallucinates a phone number.

**3. The owner sees the card within seconds.**
From the moment the call ends to the moment the email lands, the target is under 30 seconds. The card contains: caller name, callback number, reason for the call, urgency level, and a suggested reply the owner can copy or adapt.

## Dashboard concept

The dashboard looks and feels like a tape deck. Each recorded call is presented as a cassette card. The visual language is warm, tactile, and nostalgic while the information is precise and actionable.

## Hackathon context

Afterbeep is an entry in the Kiroween 2026 hackathon, Resurrection category. The concept resurrects the humble answering machine with modern AI transcription and extraction.
